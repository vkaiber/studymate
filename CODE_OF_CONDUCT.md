# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the StudyMate project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and discuss ideas, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, insults, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and other hostile conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may restrict participation when this Code of Conduct is violated. To report an issue, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub.
